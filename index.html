A motion layout library for a statistics course

Brief
Lecture videos full of formulas, tables and charts needed to look consistent across every lesson, and be fast to produce.
Idea
Design the on-screen graphics once, as a system: reusable animated layouts the editors drop in instead of building each slide from scratch.
I made
14 layout families with 63 animated variants: definition, formula, worked example, chart, table, Venn, tree, procedure steps, bento split, lower thirds and more, each with its own build-in animation.

After Effects
